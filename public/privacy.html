Privacy policy

App version 1.0 · Updated 18 October 2026

Quranflect is free. It has no advertisements, analytics, accounts or subscriptions.

On your device

Bookmarks, reading position, appearance and reading preferences are stored locally. The complete Arabic Qur’an and included English and Hindi translations work offline. Downloaded cards and additional translations remain available offline. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes this local data.

Content connections

The app checks the public GitHub Pages card feed when opened and when an enabled new-card notification arrives. Additional translations are downloaded from QuranEnc.com when selected. These services receive ordinary connection information, including your IP address, as needed to serve content. We do not operate an account system or collect reading activity.

Optional notifications

Notifications are off until you enable them. Firebase Cloud Messaging then uses a Firebase installation identifier and messaging token to deliver new-card updates. Quranflect does not include Firebase Analytics or export delivery metrics. You can turn notifications off in Settings; the app stops displaying notifications immediately and requests token deletion when connected.

Sharing and downloads

Sharing opens Android’s share sheet with the original card. Downloading opens Android’s file picker. The card’s original attribution and reference are retained. The destination you select handles the shared or downloaded file under its own policies.

Feedback

Share feedback opens your email application with the developer address. Nothing is sent until you send the email. If you contact us, we use the information you provide to respond to your request.
